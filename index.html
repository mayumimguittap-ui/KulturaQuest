About KulturaQuest

Technology that supports tradition.

KulturaQuest is a digital learning platform concept that complements—not replaces—traditional art education. It brings together cultural documentation, guided learning, creative exploration, and physical art-making.

Our focus is Philippine Visual Arts, with special attention to Ilocos Norte and the Ilocano community as reliable local resources become available.

02 · Learn

Traditional art techniques

Use these learning prompts to organize your demonstrations. Add your group's own photographs or videos after documenting the process with permission.

Learning guide 01

Observe the materials

Identify the tools and materials used. What are they made from? Where do they come from? Are there safety considerations?

Learning guide 02

Study the process

Break a technique into stages: preparation, making, finishing, and care. Record each stage with captions or approved images.

Learning guide 03

Understand the meaning

Research the artwork's context and meaning. Confirm interpretations with reliable sources or knowledgeable practitioners.

Content note: KulturaQuest should not present an unverified tutorial as an authentic traditional practice. Ask an artist, artisan, educator, or cultural knowledge holder to review locally specific information.

06 · Be responsible

Sources and acknowledgements

Every artwork, quotation, historical statement, photograph, and artist profile added to KulturaQuest must have a source and appropriate permission or license.

Before publishing content

  • Record the author, title, source, and date accessed.
  • Check image copyright and reuse permissions.
  • Ask permission before publishing interview recordings, names, or photographs.
  • Respect community protocols and sensitive cultural knowledge.
  • Have an educator or appropriate cultural adviser review content.

Add your actual bibliography and image credits here. Do not leave placeholder citations in the final school submission.
